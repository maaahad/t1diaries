Design a responsive web app called T1Diaries �� a publishing and reference platform for
            the type 1 diabetes community. Desktop-first at 1440px with mobile at 390px. Light mode only for v1.

            PRODUCT IN ONE LINE
            Four surfaces in one calm, credible product: personal Stories from parents and people living with T1D;
            Signal, a human-curated feed of device and research news; Devices, a sourced reference for CGMs, pumps and
            automated insulin delivery systems; and Numbers, interactive charts of T1D epidemiology.

            AUDIENCE
            1. A parent four weeks after their child's diagnosis, reading on a phone at 2am, exhausted and scared.
            2. An adult with T1D comparing hardware and following what is coming next.
            3. A clinician, researcher or advocate who wants primary sources without wading through supplement
            advertising.

            TONE
            Clinical calm, not corporate warmth. Confident and quiet. No stock photography of smiling people holding
            fruit, no "journey" language, no inspirational overlays, no red alarm styling. The product's warmth comes
            from the writing, so the interface should get out of its way. Every factual claim visibly carries its
            source.

            VISUAL LANGUAGE
            Borrow from the ambulatory glucose profile report that every reader already knows: a horizontal target
            corridor, a thin continuous trace, dashed threshold lines, small monospace axis labels. Use that corridor
            motif as a section divider and as the progress and status device — never as decoration on unrelated content.

            COLOR TOKENS
            paper #EFF2F6 page background
            card #FFFFFF surfaces
            ink #0E141B primary text
            muted #5B6674 secondary text
            faint #8A94A2 labels, axis text
            rule #D5DBE3 1px borders
            trace #4A2FE7 primary action, links, data line
            traceSoft #EDE9FE selected states, chips
            band #CDEBD8 in-range fill, success
            bandLine #2E9E63 in-range stroke, confirmed states
            high #E8A33D caution, unverified, preprint badge
            low #D9455F destructive, error, safety-critical
            Do not introduce gradients. One accent only.

            TYPOGRAPHY
            Display: Bricolage Grotesque, 700/800, tight tracking (-2% to -4%). Used for page titles and section
            headings only.
            Body: IBM Plex Sans, 400/500/600. Body copy 16px/1.62, long-form story text 18px/1.7 at a 68-character
            measure.
            Utility: IBM Plex Mono, 400/500. Feature IDs, dates, source names, axis labels, badge text, all-caps
            eyebrows at 11px with 0.12em letter-spacing.
            Type scale: 48 / 34 / 24 / 20 / 17 / 15 / 13 / 11.

            LAYOUT
            1180px max content width, 12-column grid, 24px gutters. Long-form reading column 680px. 8px spacing base.
            Border radius 10px on cards, 20px on pills, 5px on buttons. Borders instead of shadows; a single soft shadow
            reserved for overlays only.

            SCREENS TO DESIGN
            1. Home — one lead story, a Signal strip of six items, a featured chart, entry points to Devices and
            Numbers.
            2. Story reader — title, byline with the author's relationship to T1D, content-warning interstitial state,
            reading progress, "this helped me" reaction, related stories, comments.
            3. Story editor — Markdown surface with live preview, autosave indicator, tag pickers for
            audience/topic/devices, content-warning toggles, cover image, publish sheet.
            4. Signal feed — filter rail for category, source type and date; cards showing headline, editor summary,
            source, date, evidence badge, save control.
            5. Signal item — headline, two-sentence summary, evidence badges, other outlets covering the same story,
            prominent outbound link to the original.
            6. Editorial queue (admin) — dense triage list with keyboard shortcuts, bulk select, inline summary editing,
            source health strip.
            7. Device directory — filterable grid with category, region and compatibility facets.
            8. Device detail — sourced spec table where every value shows its citation and last-verified date,
            compatibility section, related news.
            9. Compare devices — up to three columns with differences highlighted.
            10. Numbers explorer — choropleth plus time series, filter controls, methodology panel, export menu, and a
            "view as table" state.
            11. Search results — grouped by stories, news and devices.
            12. Profile and reading list.
            13. Sign-up and sign-in.
            14. Moderation console.

            COMPONENTS TO BUILD AS A LIBRARY
            Buttons (primary, secondary, ghost, destructive), pill and badge set (priority, evidence type, verification
            state, category), input, textarea, select, combobox, tag picker, card (story, news, device, chart), table
            with sticky header, filter chip row, cursor-paginated list with loading skeletons, empty state, error state,
            toast, modal, interstitial overlay, tab bar, breadcrumb, pagination, avatar, byline, citation footnote,
            source-health indicator, chart frame with title, source line and export menu.

            STATES REQUIRED FOR EVERY COMPONENT
            Default, hover, focus-visible (2px trace-colour ring, 3px offset), active, disabled, loading, empty, error.

            WRITING RULES FOR ALL UI COPY
            Sentence case everywhere. Active voice. A button names its outcome and keeps that name through the flow:
            "Publish" produces "Published". Errors state what happened and what to do next, and never apologise. Empty
            states invite an action rather than describing absence. Unknown data reads "not published", never a blank or
            a zero.

            NON-NEGOTIABLES
            - WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 on body text, 3:1 on large text and UI boundaries, visible focus on every interactive
            element, and a 44px minimum touch target.
            - Every chart needs an equivalent data table.
            - A persistent, unobtrusive line stating this is not medical advice, present on the page and not only in the
            footer.
            - No third-party trackers or consent banners in the design.
            - Design the loading and empty state for every list before the populated state.
